Updates: WR-614, WR-619

#### WR-614 / WR-619 (update, 2026-10-31). Candidate opt-in refinement for exact plane/line operands

- **PR:** https://github.com/fkirill/solidcore/pull/2 (stacked on that fork's sync PR #1).
- **Scope:** the owned `boolean::api_brep` union, intersection and difference entry points, including the options/curves wrappers that share their implementation. This is an original precision policy, not a port of a fork fix and not a general repair of proto-topology's band resolution.
- **Flag:** `Kernel::fixes.boolean_refine_planar_strips`, off by default. Before constructing the merge, exact plane/line solids with nonparallel faces crossing within the sum of their face tolerances may receive a smaller entity tolerance. The proposed precision is one sixteenth of the smallest resolved crossing-plane distance, bounded below by four coordinate-scaled roundoff allowances. Before any imprint edits a source edge, eligible analytic line/plane and coplanar line/line vertex definitions are placed at their analytic roots; roots outside source intervals, parallel pairs, skew gaps and moves beyond the definition tolerance are left alone. The ordinary intersector and merge then continue. Geometry, ids and attribute stores are retained; result tolerances can be smaller. Differential comparison tolerances are unchanged.
- **Eligibility:** both inputs must be manifold solids with analytic plane faces and 3D line edges. All line endpoints and incident face planes must agree with the vertex geometry within `128 * f64::EPSILON * max_coordinate`. Existing UV trims must be lines or nonrational degree-one B-splines whose lifted span endpoints match the edge within the same allowance. Curved/gapped geometry, parallel or merely touching faces, nonfinite geometry/tolerances, and a crossing indistinguishable from roundoff are not refined. Ordinary overlaps keep their precision. B-spline representations of planes/lines are not recognized by this first implementation.
- **Native evidence:** all 24 existing `wr614` fixture cases compare exactly against pinned SMLib 1.0.1 (`PASS boolean.api_brep cases=24 passed=24 failed=0 deferred=0 max_rel_err=0e0`). This confirms the existing failures/broken bodies; it does not validate the opt-in construction. There is no corrected native reference.
- **Candidate construction:** at slope 0.1, both d=5e-6 and d=1e-5 build manifold unions (11 F/27 E/18 V), intersections (5/9/6), and differences (9/21/14). The original default union still has no solid region at d=5e-6 and errors at d=1e-5.
- **Geometry regression:** the expanded test exercises both operand orders, slopes 0.01/0.1/0.3, and d=2e-6/5e-6/1e-5/1.5e-5, with level-2 B-rep validation and a strict 1e-9 relative analytic-volume assertion. All 72 combinations pass. Union/difference use the mass query; the tiny triangular intersection is measured directly from its boundary vertices because the mass integrator has a separate loss of precision (see `plan/findings/mass.integrator--thin-prism-volume-roundoff.md`). The direct volume comparison retains the same 1e-9 relative threshold. No existing case, comparison tolerance or unit status changed.
- **Validation:** all four affected native units pass 4,020 cases and exact-base coverage checks; formatting, workspace Clippy, wasm, six eligibility/preservation guards and the 72-case regression pass on ARM64. The broad ARM results and same-base test controls are recorded in `plan/findings/general--arm64-validation-of-1794.md`. Final Ubuntu x86-64 validation is in progress; the PR remains a draft.

Updates: WR-614, WR-619

#### WR-614 / WR-619 (update, 2026-10-31). Candidate opt-in refinement for exact plane/line operands

- **PR:** https://github.com/fkirill/solidcore/pull/2 (stacked on that fork's sync PR #1).
- **Scope:** the owned `boolean::api_brep` union, intersection and difference entry points, including the options/curves wrappers that share their implementation. This is an original precision policy, not a port of a fork fix and not a general repair of proto-topology's band resolution.
- **Flag:** `Kernel::fixes.boolean_refine_planar_strips`, off by default. Before constructing the merge, exact plane/line solids with nonparallel faces crossing within the sum of their face tolerances may receive a smaller entity tolerance. The proposed precision is one sixteenth of the smallest resolved crossing-plane distance, bounded below by four coordinate-scaled roundoff allowances. The ordinary intersector, imprint and merge then run unchanged. Geometry, ids and attribute stores are retained; result tolerances can be smaller. Differential comparison tolerances are unchanged.
- **Eligibility:** both inputs must be manifold solids with analytic plane faces and 3D line edges. All line endpoints and incident face planes must agree with the vertex geometry within `128 * f64::EPSILON * max_coordinate`. Existing UV trims must be affine lines with sampled lifts matching the edge within the same allowance. Curved/gapped geometry, parallel or merely touching faces, nonfinite geometry/tolerances, and a crossing indistinguishable from roundoff are not refined. Ordinary overlaps keep their precision. B-spline representations of planes/lines are not recognized by this first implementation.
- **Native evidence:** all 24 existing `wr614` fixture cases compare exactly against pinned SMLib 1.0.1 (`PASS boolean.api_brep cases=24 passed=24 failed=0 deferred=0 max_rel_err=0e0`). This confirms the existing failures/broken bodies; it does not validate the opt-in construction. There is no corrected native reference.
- **Candidate construction:** at slope 0.1, both d=5e-6 and d=1e-5 build manifold unions (11 F/27 E/18 V), intersections (5/9/6), and differences (9/21/14). The original default union still has no solid region at d=5e-6 and errors at d=1e-5.
- **Open validation:** the expanded test exercises both operand orders, slopes 0.01/0.1/0.3, and d=2e-6/5e-6/1e-5/1.5e-5, with level-2 B-rep validation and a strict 1e-9 relative analytic-volume assertion. A d=2e-6, slope=0.1 intersection currently measures 8.07999997759174908e-11 versus 8.07999998790584863e-11 from the representable input depth (relative discrepancy ~1.28e-9). This assertion is still failing; no tolerance was loosened. The PR is not ready until the numerical cause is resolved or the candidate is withdrawn.
